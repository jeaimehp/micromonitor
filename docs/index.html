A little desk display for your Mac

µMonitor turns a Particle Xenon and an Adafruit 3.5" TFT FeatherWing into a touchscreen system monitor. A menu bar app on the Mac feeds it over USB every 2 seconds, and the display has its own touch menu. No soldering: the Xenon plugs into the back of the FeatherWing.

Live graphs

CPU and GPU utilization, RAM, disk read/write and usage, network rx/tx, the top 5 processes, and a big clock.

Touch control

Tap to open the menu on the display. Every setting is mirrored in the µMonitor menu bar app, both ways.

12 themes, 8 layouts

Dark, Light, Retro Green, Amber, Synthwave, plus full-screen LCARS, Tron, Windows XP and System 7 layouts. See them all, or make your own.

Photos and timers

A photo slideshow streamed from your Mac, a mixed photo + dashboard view, and a timer and stopwatch.

Build one

You need a Particle Xenon, an Adafruit 3.5" TFT FeatherWing (the V1, STMPE610 touch version), a micro-USB data cable and a Mac. The documentation covers parts, assembly, flashing and installing the app. No Xenon? See the ESP32 notes (untested).
